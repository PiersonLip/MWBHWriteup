# Flowchart path blurbs

These show in the **How do Galactic black holes form?** detail panel when you click a path.
Edit the paragraph under each heading, then run `python3 scripts/apply_vault.py`.

## merger · Stellar mergers

Two normal stars merge (often after a failed CE), then the leftover collapses to a BH. Biggest isolated channel.

## disrupt · Disrupted binaries

The binary comes unbound at the first or second collapse when the kick and/or mass loss push the energy positive.

## single · Initially single

Born without a companion under the Moe+17 mass-dependent binary fraction. Mostly low-mass BHs.

## bhstar-m · BH+star mergers

The BH survives the kick, then later merges with its companion after unstable MT or a failed CE.

## dco-m · DCO mergers

Rare double-compact mergers within 10 Gyr. Among the channels that can reach ≳30–50 M☉ remnants at solar Z.

## bhbh · Detached BH-BH

Dominant surviving binary class: stable-MT (broad, lower-mass) plus the wind-driven ~13 M☉ channel.

## otherbin · BH + other

BH+WD, BH+NS, and BH+star combined. Rare, but basically the main current observational windows.
