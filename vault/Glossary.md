# Glossary

Hover tips used on the site. Edit carefully — re-export may reshuffle.

## Blaauw effect
Losing large quantities of mass can dramatically shift the center of mass of a binary, widening or unbinding its orbit. Named after Adriaan Blaauw.

## common envelope
A phase of binary star evolution where both stars share one giant envelope. The drag caused by the stars orbiting through this envelope greatly reduces their orbits, sometimes leading to mergers  

## envelope
The outer "shell" of a star where gas sits, *no* fusion occurs here

## core collapse
The end of a massive star where the iron core collapses. This leaves a neutron star, a black hole, or sometimes nothing.

## DCO
Double compact object: BH-BH, BH-NS, NS-NS, or similar pairs. If they are tight enough, gravitational waves can merge them.

## Detached
Not transferring mass right now. The two objects orbit quietly without an accretion disk.

## fallback
Material that falls back onto the newborn compact object. More fallback means a weaker kick.

## Gaia
ESA's space observatory that measures the precise positions and motions of stars. Great at finding quiet black holes tugging on a visible companion.

## gala
A Python package for Galactic dynamics. We use it to integrate orbits in a Milky Way potential.

## IMF
Initial mass function. How common stars of different birth masses are. Heavier stars are rarer.

## LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA
Gravitational-wave detectors. They catch black hole mergers outside our Galaxy.

## mass transfer
One star dumps gas onto the other through the inner Lagrange point when it fills its Roche lobe.

## MESA
Modules for Experiments in Stellar Astrophysics. A highly accurate simulation code which simulates the evolution of single or binary stars.

## metallicity
How metal-rich the gas is (where metals are everything heavier than helium). More metals means stronger stellar winds.

## microlens
When a massive object briefly bends and brightens light from a background star. The light curve can reveal the lens mass even if the lens is dark.

## natal kick
An extra shove that a newborn neutron star or black hole can get from an asymmetric supernova.

## pair-instability gap
A predicted range of BH masses where the star blows itself apart before it can make a remnant.

## POSYDON
A binary population synthesis code built on detailed MESA stellar evolution grids.

## Roche-lobe overflow
When a star expands past the potential which fill without dumping mass onto its companion. This causes gas to to be transferred from one star to the other.

## Roman
NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope. Expected to find large quanties of black holes through gravitational microlensing.

## stable mass transfer
Mass transfer that in a state of equilibrium, leading to more long term transfer of mass instead of a merger.

## Wolf-Rayet
Hot, helium-rich stars that have already lost their hydrogen envelopes. These stars have incredibly strong winds

## Winds
Material which is ejected from the outer atmosphere of a star into the surrounding area.

## X-ray binaries
A binary where a black hole (or neutron star) steals gas from a companion. This in-falling gas heats up and shines in X-rays.

## ZAMS
Zero-age main sequence. The initial common birth-state of a star, when it first begins hydrogen fusion in its core
