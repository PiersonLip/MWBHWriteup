# α<sub>CE</sub> doesn't make black holes. It decides who survives.

*06 · Common envelopes*

> Edit the blocks below. Interactive figure guts stay on the site; only captions / titles / hints sync.

:::prose
Unstable mass transfer can dunk the binary into a [common envelope]{tip: A phase of binary star evolution where both stars share one giant envelope. The drag caused by the stars orbiting through this envelope greatly reduces their orbits, sometimes leading to mergers}. The efficiency α<sub>CE</sub> is the fraction of orbital energy used to blow that envelope off. Lower α means a deeper inspiral: more mergers, tighter survivors, and a harder time for unequal-mass systems.

In the paper's runs, dialing α<sub>CE</sub> from 1.0 to 0.5 to 0.1 boosts stellar mergers, BH+star mergers, and especially DCO mergers (BH-BH pulling ahead of BH-NS). Detached BH-BH counts tick up a bit, while BH+NS/WD/star detached systems drop. The BH mass peaks barely budge. CE rearranges *who is bound to whom*, not how heavy the remnants are.
:::
