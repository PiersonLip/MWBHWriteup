# Fancy binaries, simple Galaxy

*02 · Methodology*

> Edit the blocks below. Interactive figure guts stay on the site; only captions / titles / hints sync.

:::prose
In order to model all of these binaries evolving in our galaxy, we use POSYDON, which takes single stars and binaries from birth ([[ZAMS]]) all the way to their death over the scale of billions of years. We use a selection of simulations of **10 million binaries** at varying [[metallicity|metallicities]][^1] and scale their results up to a Milky Way-like galaxy. 

With these systems there is still a large amount of physics we don't fully understand, so we try variations and see what sticks best. However, a good chunk of the physics stays the same between simulations: we use a a Kroupa-like [[IMF]] for the primary stars, POSYDON's wind and mixing setup, and the usual [[common envelope]] with the standard process.
